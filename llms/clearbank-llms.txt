# ClearBank

> ClearBank is a UK clearing and Banking-as-a-Service bank (founded 2015, first new UK clearing bank in 250+ years), authorised by the PRA and regulated by the FCA and PRA (FRN 754568). It is a direct participant in Faster Payments, CHAPS and Bacs and exposes Agency, Embedded and Transaction Banking through a single token-authenticated, RSA-signed JSON REST API — the ClearBank Financial Institutions API. Authentication is a bearer API token plus a DigitalSignature (RSA-SHA256) header over the request body, with a mandatory X-Request-Id idempotency key (24h duplicate window). Payment initiation is asynchronous (202 Accepted) with outcomes delivered over signed webhooks.

## APIs
- [GBP Accounts API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Open and manage sterling accounts, balances, transactions and reporting.
- [Faster Payments API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Initiate and return UK Faster Payments (FPS).
- [CHAPS API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): High-value CHAPS RTGS institution and customer payments (v6).
- [Multi-Currency Accounts API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/multi-currency/): Create and manage multi-currency (MCCY) and virtual accounts.
- [Multi-Currency Payments API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/multi-currency/multi-currency-payments/): Orchestrate MCCY inbound/outbound payments.
- [FX API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Spot and RFQ foreign-exchange trades.
- [SEPA Credit Transfer API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Send/receive/recall SEPA CT euro payments.
- [Confirmation of Payee API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Outbound CoP name verification (Pay.UK).
- [Customer Due Diligence (KYC) API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/api/): Retail customer onboarding checks.
- [Cross-Border Sterling API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/gbp-payments/gbp-cross-border/): Cross-border sterling payments.
- [Retail Embedded Banking Customers API](https://clearbank.github.io/uk/docs/embedded-banking/retail-customers/): Manage retail customers, accounts, savings and ISAs.
